Re:TUI community rules

Version 2026-10-10. Accept in Launcher before contributing.

Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, illegal content, impersonation, spam or sharing private information. Never post secrets or passwords.

Report content or its author from Message actions. Locally block authors to hide their posts. Send private moderation requests from the community menu. dvil is the only moderator and may remove content or ban accounts. The operator reviews reports; this is an indie project, not a 24/7 emergency service.

Privacy and retention

The community is server-readable, not end-to-end encrypted. General messages expire 24 hours after posting. Accounts and forum discussions persist until deletion. Reports and private moderation evidence expire six calendar months after creation, even if the source account/content is deleted. Private selective recovery snapshots use a seven-day rotation; expired copies are removed on the next hourly run. They exclude chat, reports, requests, presence and mention bodies. No off-site backup is configured.

Unopened device caches are pruned on next access; this is not forensic secure erasure. Provider snapshots are outside our direct deletion controls. No analytics, email login, push service, private messages or automatic password recovery is included.

Delete your account without Launcher. Account deletion also removes authored content and replies on authored discussions; moderation evidence keeps its original expiry.
