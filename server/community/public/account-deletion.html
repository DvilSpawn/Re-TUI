Delete your Re:TUI community account

This works without installing Launcher. Enter your community username and current password. Credentials are sent over HTTPS only to this community service, not stored in this page.

Deletion removes your account, authored General messages, discussions and replies. Replies by other people on your discussions are also removed. It cannot be undone. Launcher settings are unaffected.

Private reports and moderation evidence retain their original six-calendar-month expiry. Private recovery snapshots use a seven-day rotation; expired copies are removed on the next hourly run. Restoring a snapshot must apply account deletions. No off-site recovery is configured.

If you no longer have the password, request deletion verification through Re:TUI support. Do not publish passwords or personal information in an issue. The operator will arrange private verification; a username alone is not proof of ownership. There is no automatic password recovery.
