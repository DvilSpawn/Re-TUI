# Clouds artwork license review

Checked 2026-10-11 against the maintainer-supplied links:

- [Eight-sky pack used by Launcher](https://craftpix.net/freebies/free-sky-with-clouds-background-pixel-art-set/).
- [Four-background itch.io pack](https://free-game-assets.itch.io/free-pixel-art-cloud-and-sky-backgrounds).
- [CraftPix Freebie terms, sections 2.1 and 2.2](https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/).

The eight-sky set matches the bundled 37 PNG layers and their existing recorded
origin. The four-background listing is a different pack, not proof of the eight
skies' license. Both point to CraftPix's terms.

The Freebie terms permit personal/commercial projects, modification and inclusion
in applications. They restrict art-source resale and app-based saving/export of
reusable artwork. The itch.io publisher also prohibits standalone asset
redistribution. Launcher renders these layers without an image-export feature.

Assessment: commercial in-app rendering is supported by the published terms.
They do not explicitly address public Git inclusion of reusable PNGs or grant
GPL asset sublicensing. Neither listing closes that source-distribution question.
This is an evidence-based risk assessment, not a legal adjudication or an assertion
that project-source inclusion is explicitly forbidden in all circumstances.
